3. What we sell: five practices, one method

Every engagement runs on the AcademicOS method (diagnose → design processes → embed cadence → measure). That keeps delivery repeatable and the book becomes the sales brochure.

4. How clients buy: the offer ladder

Low-risk entry, then step up. The paid Diagnostic is the workhorse: fixed scope, fixed fee, AI-assisted evidence review, and it sells the Programme.

6. How we deliver: operating model

People

  • Founder: sells, leads diagnostics, signs off
  • 1–2 associates (analysts) from month 4
  • Bench: ex-Deans, retired peer-team members, domain experts on per-diem

AI leverage

  • Claude skills for SSR / evidence mapping, gap scoring, report drafts
  • Target: diagnostic in 2 weeks with 1 analyst
  • Itself a selling point: “we use what we teach”

Guardrails

  • Written independence policy: never consult where you assess or sit on a board
  • No “guaranteed grade” promises
  • Client data: NDA + local storage
