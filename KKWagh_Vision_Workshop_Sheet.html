Before you start

A vision is a destination 10–15 years out, not a list of activities. This sheet takes the team from who we are to one tested sentence in six short rounds. Run it in a 90-minute workshop, projected, one facilitator typing.

  1. IdentityPick what the university will be known for, who it serves, where, and what changes by 2040.
  2. TensionsEach member votes on five trade-offs. Spread of votes shows where the team really disagrees.
  3. ComposeBuild the sentence from slots or start from a preset. Keep it to 40 words or fewer.
  4. Stress-testAuto-checks for length, clichés, place and horizon, then six judgement tests.
  5. ShortlistSave versions, vote with +1, pick the final.
  6. CascadeDraft mission, values and 2030 goals. Print a one-page summary.
The tension to name: a vision that every university could sign says nothing. The test is whether a rival would refuse to sign it.
