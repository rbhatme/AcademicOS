## 2. Extra dimensions for Jev to examine
1. **Assessment & evidence**: rubric, pass standard, artefact/portfolio. Without it "skill" is unverifiable.
2. **Proficiency ladder**: Level 1 to 3 across semesters; avoid repeating the same skill.
3. **Industry validation & certification**: co-design with employers; map to NSQF / Agriculture Skill Council QPs / FSSAI FoSTaC / NSDC; badge or certificate value.
4. **Capacity & infrastructure**: equipment per student, batch size, lab hours, consumables cost, maintenance.
5. **Safety, compliance, ethics**: FSSAI, biosafety / IBSC, animal and insect handling, chemical disposal.
6. **Economics of the skill**: unit cost, margin, break-even; can a student earn from it?
7. **Entrepreneurship pathway**: incubation, micro-unit, market linkage, finance access.
8. **Seasonality & real-world exposure**: crop calendar vs semester; live farm / plant / internship, not simulation.
9. **Digital & AI layer**: sensors, drones, data logging, Excel/AI for analysis, traceability.
10. **Faculty capability**: has the teacher done the skill commercially? Industry co-trainers?
11. **Cross-school integration**: Agri Business feasibility on Food Tech / Agri / Biotech products; shared capstone.
12. **Transferable skills**: communication, teamwork, documentation, problem solving, English.
13. **Local demand evidence**: Nashik / Maharashtra employer and market data per skill.
14. **Outcome tracking**: placement, self-employment, income after 1-3 years; feeds annual revision.
15. **Sustainability / ESG**: waste, water, energy, circularity.
16. **Inclusion**: access for women and rural-first-generation learners; language.

## 3. School reports

### 3.1 College of Agricultural Engineering & Technology
Submitted: 2 courses (Operation & Maintenance of Farm Machinery; Hydro-meteorological Instruments), each a 10-criterion, 4-level rubric (40 marks).

**Strengths**
- Only school with a real assessment instrument and competency bands.
- Skills are verbs of performance: install, operate, diagnose, calibrate, repair.
- Safety and record-keeping are scored.

**Gaps**
- Rubric is generic: "with guidance / minor assistance" grades teacher help, not output quality. No measurable standards (e.g., seed-rate error within X%, calibration within Y%).
- Only 2 courses; no precision agriculture, drones, irrigation/fertigation, solar pumps, CAD/GIS, PLC/IoT, farm electrification, repair-shop entrepreneurship.
- No named machines, tasks or tools; no industry partner (tractor/implement OEM, dealer); no certification (ASCI/NSQF).
- Viva and "technical knowledge" are 10-20% of marks; weight should be practical.
- Weak career narrative (service technician, dealer, custom-hiring centre).

**Fix**
1. Replace help-based descriptors with outcome thresholds and timed tasks.
2. Add 3-4 courses: drone/precision ag, irrigation & fertigation systems, solar/energy, farm-machinery custom-hire business.
3. Tie to OEM / dealer trainers; aim for an ASCI-aligned certificate.
4. Add a capstone: diagnose and fix a faulty machine in a live workshop; log it.

### 3.2 College of Agriculture Business Management
Submitted: 8 SECs x 4 skills (Computer apps, Beneficial insects, Agribusiness proposal, Bio-agents, Seed, Soil & water, Post-harvest, Nursery). Statements only.

**Strengths**
- Good spread; several have clear enterprise potential (seed, nursery, bio-agents).
- Agribusiness Proposal and Seed Marketing are the right ABM-specific core.

**Gaps**
- Skills are "develop the ability to..." statements. No tool, output, standard or assessment named. Hardest to defend against your hands-on and rigour tests.
- Overlaps heavily with the College of Agriculture list (insects, bio-agents, seed, nursery, post-harvest). Identity is unclear: why are business students doing lab production?
- Business skills are the genuine differentiator but are the least specified: no named tools (Excel, Tally, GST, e-NAM, FPO / APMC processes), no live client.
- No outcomes, teachers or semesters given.

**Fix**
1. Cut to 4-5 courses that are business-led: Agribusiness Proposal, Digital Farm Management / Data Analysis (Excel, GIS), Agri-Marketing & Trade (e-NAM, APMC, export docs, GST), Agri-Finance (credit, KCC, insurance, subsidy schemes), Value-chain / FPO management.
2. Keep production skills only where they feed a business case; run them jointly with the Agriculture college.
3. Each skill: define a deliverable (e.g., a bankable DPR for a real farmer, costed to NABARD format; farmer survey of 50 respondents).
4. Add rubric, live-client partner (FPO, mandi trader, agri-input dealer), and project defence before a panel.

### 3.3 College of Biotechnology
Submitted: 6 SECs, sem I-IV, skill lists plus outcome statements, teachers named. Strongest documentation.

**Strengths**
- Technically rigorous: tissue culture, DNA isolation, PCR, cloning, bioinformatics, markers.
- Progressive by semester; common aseptic / safety / documentation core.
- Outcomes include troubleshooting and interpretation (rigour, not recipes).

**Gaps**
- Repetition: DNA isolation (SEC-112, 235), PCR (112, 123, 246), gel electrophoresis (112, 123, 235). No ladder showing what is new each time.
- Employability claims ("entry-level roles") are generic; no named employers, labs, or job titles/pay.
- Bioinformatics lacks named tools (BLAST, Clustal, MEGA); no qPCR, sequencing, CRISPR, ELISA, or analytical QC (HPLC); no GLP / ISO 17025 exposure.
- Cost and consumables for molecular work not addressed; batch sizes unknown.
- Entrepreneurship route missing (tissue-culture unit, testing lab). SEC-246 lacks code; course numbering inconsistent.
- No assessment rubric.

**Fix**
1. Build a ladder: Sem I technique -> II optimise -> III design -> IV apply on a real sample.
2. Name tools and add GLP / documentation standard; add one analytical-QC or qPCR module.
3. Partner with 2-3 tissue-culture units / diagnostic labs; student-led micro-propagation unit as income model.
4. Add practical exam with unseen sample and troubleshooting task; log reproducibility.

### 3.4 College of Agriculture (B.Sc. Agri)
Submitted: 9 SECs x 5 skills; outcomes for most; teachers named.

**Strengths**
- Broadest set with real enterprise flavour: nursery, mushroom, beneficial insects, bio-agents, seed, vermicompost, Jeevamrut.
- Most outcomes ask for a product: pure culture, spawn, seedlings, compost; unit setup in 3 courses.
- High student engagement potential; low capital to start (mushroom, vermicompost, nursery).

**Defects to fix now**
- **Biofertilizer & Biopesticide, Skills 4 and 5** ("The specific steps for Gram staining..."; "How mass production of biofertilizers works...") are leftover prompt text from an AI draft, not skills. Replace.
- **Video Production** is generic digital media, unrelated to agriculture. Reframe as agri-communication (farmer-advisory videos, product marketing) or move to a common pool.
- Biofertilizer outcome statements missing (no outcome block seen for that SEC).
- Mixed quality of writing: some outcomes use inflated language ("mastery", "advanced", "thousands of litres") that is not credible for a teaching lab (Post-harvest: scale-up from lab to thousands of litres, canning plant; confirm equipment really exists).

**Gaps**
- Overlap with ABM and Food Tech (post-harvest, canning, jams) and with Biotech (tissue culture, aseptic techniques): share labs and trainers.
- No assessment rubric; no minimum standard (e.g., "spawn with <5% contamination", "90% graft take").
- Employers/markets not named; FSSAI licensing for processed products not mentioned.
- Seasonality not scheduled.

**Fix**
1. Correct the defects above this month.
2. Add measurable success thresholds to every skill.
3. Offer a combined "Agri-enterprise" capstone: run one unit for a semester, with sales and a P&L; coordinate with ABM.
4. Add FSSAI hygiene training for processing courses (FoSTaC).

### 3.5 College of Food Technology
Submitted: 5 SECs x 5 skills: Extrusion, Bottling & Canning, Bakery, Milling (rice, dal, spice), Drying. One-line descriptions; no outcomes, teachers, semesters or rubric.

**Strengths**
- Equipment-based and industry-realistic (extruders, retort, seamers, mills, dryers). High employability fit for processing plants.
- Process-parameter control and quality evaluation are in the right places.
- Logical unit-operation structure.

**Gaps**
- Skill = machine operation. Rigour (food science: water activity, heat-penetration, F0 value, HACCP, shelf-life) mostly absent from the text. "Understanding spray and freeze drying" is not hands-on.
- No outcome statements, no assessment, no teachers/semester; weakest documentation of the five.
- Missing core food-industry skills: FSSAI / HACCP / GMP / FoSTaC, sensory evaluation, product development, packaging and shelf-life study, lab QC (microbiology, proximate analysis), costing.
- Machine access: can every student actually operate twin-screw, retort, freeze dryer? Capacity and batch constraints unstated.
- No industry partners or certifications; no entrepreneurship (food micro-enterprise, FSSAI licence, PMFME scheme).

**Fix**
1. Add outcome statements, with rubric: process record, product spec, and sensory/lab result.
2. Add a common compulsory layer: HACCP/GMP + FoSTaC certificate; shelf-life study; costing.
3. Make each course end with a product that meets a spec (e.g., puffed snack at target expansion ratio, moisture, and sensory score).
4. Partner with 2-3 plants for internship / trainer days; PMFME-linked incubation.
5. Where equipment is limited, use industry-visit simulations and label as such.

## 4. Cross-cutting recommendations for all schools
1. **One template**: Skill -> task -> tool -> output -> standard -> assessment -> job role.
2. **Common rubric** (4 levels with measurable thresholds, practical >= 70% of marks).
3. **Employer / market anchor** named for each skill; review annually.
4. **Shared pool** for overlapping skills (aseptic, post-harvest, nursery, video, data/Excel/GIS), taught once and credited across schools.
5. **Certification path** (ASCI/NSQF, FoSTaC, GLP) so skills are portable.
6. **Capstone / micro-enterprise** per school with P&L; ABM students as business partners.
7. **Quality gate** before submission: remove AI-draft remnants; check every skill is observable and measurable.
8. **Track outcomes** (placement, self-employment) and revise.
